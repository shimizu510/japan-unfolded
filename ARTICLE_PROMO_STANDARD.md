# Article promotion standard

Every new culture article must include the existing desktop `yuji-rail` and mobile `mobile-brand-bar`, with Yuji's portrait and these two promises:

Creature-inspiration features must grade claims as documented creator testimony, strong comparison, or editorial interpretation. Official Pokédex categories describe the fictional taxonomy and must not be presented as zoological diagnoses. Do not turn visual similarity or fan etymology into a confirmed design origin. Use licensed real-animal photography, official text links, and original non-imitative diagrams; never generate or redraw protected characters as substitutes for official art.

Food-culture features must distinguish a widespread pattern from a universal rule, identify regional or shop-specific systems, explain the practical workflow behind visible etiquette, and include dietary, allergy, accessibility, queueing and payment limits. Never turn optional slurping, finishing soup, returning a bowl or specialist ordering vocabulary into a test of cultural worth. Actual food and shop photographs require visible creator, source, license and modification credits.

Sushi-history features must distinguish fermented narezushi, shortened-fermentation nama-nare, vinegared hayazushi, pressed regional forms and Edo nigiri rather than presenting sushi as one linear invention. Treat Southeast Asian or Chinese origin routes and single-inventor stories as debated when the evidence is not conclusive. Explain that early nigiri toppings were often cured, marinated, boiled or grilled; do not define historic sushi as raw fish. Include raw-seafood and home-fermentation safety limits, allergen variation, and visible image licenses.

Treat variant details as evidence of transmission rather than errors; original visuals must be non-imitative and clearly labeled editorial artwork.

Never encourage entry into closed property, removal of covers, leaning over shafts, dropping objects, contaminating water, or unsupervised exploration. Use official site guidance for accessible heritage wells; date-check hours, admission and restrictions.

For confrontation legends such as Kuchisake-onna, present purported escape answers as story variants—not real-world safety instructions. Everyday-life and school features must distinguish a widespread pattern from a universal rule. Name differences by municipality, school type, age, and campus where relevant; explain the educational or social system behind the visible custom; and include practical limits, safety concerns, or current reform debates instead of presenting a ritual as proof of a national personality.

Features about children travelling independently must distinguish walking to a local public school from longer private or national-school trips by train. Treat “alone” as travel without a personal escort, not evidence of travel without support. Verify current survey data and describe route practice, group commuting, school rules, transport staff, volunteers, traffic risk, weather, disability and family variation. Never encourage readers to follow or photograph identifiable children.

Destination guides must explain how history still shapes the visitor experience, provide a practical route, name realistic costs or constraints where relevant, and date-check operational details. Use multiple actual destination photographs with visible author, source, license, and modification credits; do not substitute generic Japan imagery.

Character-culture features must use official embeds, clearly licensed documentary photos, simple identification marks where legally appropriate, or original non-imitative editorial diagrams. Never generate substitutes that imitate protected characters. Separate official character facts from the publication's cultural interpretation.

Language and culture explainers must not treat `tatemae`/`honne`, indirectness, `uchi`/`soto`, or similar concepts as personality traits shared by every Japanese person. Distinguish dictionary meaning, observed speech patterns, individual variation, power, and the safety-critical situations where explicit language is required. Original diagrams must be labeled as editorial diagrams; historical and museum images need visible creator, source, and license credits.

When an article naturally introduces Japanese words, manga language, signs, conversation, or study, add one contextual link to **Japanese Training Lab** (`https://shimizu510.github.io/japanese-training-lab/`). Describe it accurately as free web practice with no signup required. Do not force the link into unrelated paragraphs or replace the required DraMap and TikTok actions.

Use the production URLs already present in the site. The desktop card must remain visible while the reader scrolls. The mobile bar must remain compact and must not obscure article text. It must always show Yuji's portrait plus both actions—`LIVE Japan` and `Learn Japanese`—including on phones 520px wide or narrower. Never hide the DraMap action to make the bar fit. Use safe-area-aware bottom spacing so the bar does not cover article text, links, embedded video controls, or the iPhone home indicator. Do not publish a new article without Yuji's face and both promises.

The homepage hero must place a visible **Read the full story →** link immediately after the featured story's introduction. Do not rely only on a linked image or a second card below the fold. On mobile, keep the title, introduction and this link before the hero image so an intrinsically tall image cannot push the action out of the first view.

The homepage must also expose Yuji, TikTok LIVE and DraMap through the shared compact promotion: `home-promo` on desktop and `mobile-brand-bar` below 1200px. Both actions must remain visible; neither may cover navigation, editorial text, media controls or the story CTA.

Reduce image height, copy or spacing before allowing either action to fall below the viewport. Verify the homepage and new article at 1366×768, at 1440×900 or larger, and at 390×844. The desktop rail must sit against the browser edge, outside the article's readable measure, with a visible gutter between card and text. If the viewport cannot fit the article, gutter and full 205px card together, switch to the mobile bar early; never let the desktop card cover copy.

Hero and story images need explicit responsive heights, `object-fit` and intentional `object-position` values. Mobile content order and image dimensions must protect the primary story CTA. Preserve Japan Unfolded's own visual identity rather than copying Japan Daily Brief.

Travel and seasonal features must state the practical trade-offs, not only the visual appeal: crowd peaks, weather hazards, transport or holiday constraints, and the date on which operational details were checked. Use actual destination photography with visible creator, source and license credits; every named season or major experience needs a representative visual. Canonical, Open Graph, sitemap and robots URLs must use the current production domain. External sources and promotional links open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.

Unusual-accommodation features must distinguish licensed lodging from time-based rest spaces or cafés, explain branch-specific and gender-specific rules, and date-check operating details. Never imply that a manga or internet café provides the privacy, security or legal status of a hotel. For temple stays, treat worship and resident religious life as the primary context—not entertainment—and verify curfews, meals, baths, accessibility and photography rules with the individual lodging.

Every new article must link to at least one existing story, and at least one relevant existing story must link back to the new article. All sitemap entries must use the GitHub Pages production domain. Publishing means committing and pushing the verified source to the existing repository, then confirming the GitHub Pages workflow and the live URLs. Do not create or use a separate hosting project.

Ranked features must state what is being ranked and why. Present countdowns from the lowest rank to the winner (for a top ten: **10 → 1**) so number one appears last; use the same order in headings, jump navigation and any structured data. A ranking should span meaningfully different kinds of value rather than padding one category with near-duplicates. Legal, medical, safety and live operational claims require current official sources.

History features must distinguish surviving fabric, archaeological remains, reconstructions and later memorial sites in captions and travel guidance. When a famous anecdote is disputed, state what primary or official evidence supports, what recent scholarship questions, and do not use a dramatic later account as settled fact. Keep all new article media within the reading measure when the 205px desktop rail is active. Use the desktop rail only at 1320px and above; 1319px and below uses the safe mobile bar so the card retains a visible gutter from article content.

Animal, mascot and railway features must distinguish the animal’s ceremonial or promotional title from operational railway work, name the human and public institutions behind any claimed “rescue,” and put animal welfare before visitor access. Duty calendars are not appearance guarantees: date-check the current holder, days off and official visiting rules, and never encourage touching, flash photography or disturbing a resting animal.

Cross-cultural features must distinguish survey results, government policy, popular-culture frames and individual experience. Never turn a country, continent or people into a personality type. If a poll asks about only selected countries, label the selection and do not present missing countries as low-ranked. Original editorial diagrams must be identified as diagrams and explain where categories overlap.

Retail comparisons must define what each source counts before comparing store totals. Use dominant-market patterns rather than national personality claims, include at least one close peer that complicates an “only in Japan” narrative, and name the labor, waste or access trade-off behind frictionless service.

Every article that loads the shared `live-status.js` also receives the shared anonymous reader discussion from `comments.js`. New article markup must keep `main .article` so the discussion mounts automatically. Participation requires no account; the optional display name defaults to **Anonymous reader**. The Cloud Function is the only write path and must retain origin checks, a 500-character limit, link limits, a honeypot, IP-based posting limits, reporting, and automatic hiding after three independent reports. Never replace this with direct public Firestore writes or expose private moderation data. Do not claim that anonymous posting means untraceable posting: the server stores a one-way IP hash for abuse control.

Historical Buddhist afterlife features must distinguish Buddhist rebirth realms from an eternal Christian-style hell, avoid treating oni as a Japanese Satan, and separate documented imagery from claims about what every Japanese person believed. Identify the specific scroll, collection, date, source text and viewing format where known. Explain conservation-driven display rotations and never imply that collection ownership guarantees an object is currently on view. Public-domain cultural-property images require visible work, collection, source and modification credits.
