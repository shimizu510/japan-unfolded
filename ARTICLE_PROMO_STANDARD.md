# Article promotion standard

Every new culture article must include the existing desktop `yuji-rail` and mobile `mobile-brand-bar`, with Yuji's portrait and these two promises:

- TikTok: **Watch LIVE Japan with Yuji**
- DraMap: **Learn Japanese while playing**

Use the production URLs already present in the site. The desktop card must remain visible while the reader scrolls. The mobile bar must remain compact and must not obscure article text. Do not publish a new article without Yuji's face and both promises.
